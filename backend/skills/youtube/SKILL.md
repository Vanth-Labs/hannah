---
name: youtube
description: Open a YouTube search in the browser
open: https://www.youtube.com/results?search_query={arg}
phrases: ["reproducí", "reproduci", "poné en youtube", "pon en youtube", "buscá en youtube"]
---
Use this when the user wants to watch or play something on YouTube.
The input is what to search for.
Example: "reproducí lofi beats" -> opens a YouTube search for "lofi beats".
